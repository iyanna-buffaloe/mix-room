Mix Room processing
Only the supplied Drums stem was further separated, using the existing local
MDX23C drum model. Every other supplied stem was retained for visualization.
The app's existing analysis logic generated the visualization frames.
Playback combines all ten aligned original stems at their supplied relative
levels, with one shared headroom gain and MP3 encoding.
