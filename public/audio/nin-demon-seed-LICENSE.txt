Mix Room processing
120,271 samples (2.72723356 seconds) of shared digital silence were removed
from every original stem with the same sample-aligned cut. The original
coupled drum stem is retained. The app's existing analysis logic generated
the visualization frames. Playback combines all thirteen aligned trimmed
original stems at their supplied relative levels, with one shared headroom
gain and MP3 encoding.

Revision 2 removes exactly one additional second (44,100 samples at
44.1 kHz) from that combined demo, then re-encodes at 320 kbps. All
visualization frame and panning times shift by the same one second. The
total opening cut is 3.72723356 seconds; no further onset trim is applied.
The remaining opening rest and natural instrument activity are retained.

Revision 3 removes exactly one further second (44,100 samples at44.1kHz)
from revision2, re-encoding at320kbps and shifting all visualization frame
and panning times by the same second. The total cut from the original
stems is4.72723356seconds. No additional onset trim or drum separation is
applied; all original combined parts and natural activity remain.
